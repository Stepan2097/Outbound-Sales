The ZM app itself doesn’t directly track deposits or registrations inside the offer. For those events to appear, the data needs to come from the affiliate network or advertiser via postback (or API integration).

Here’s how it works in practice:

Your tracker (Keitaro/Voluum/Scaleo) sends the clickID to the offer.

When a user registers or deposits, the affiliate network/advertiser fires a postback with that clickID.

The tracker catches the event and then, through the ZM app integration with the Pixel API, it passes the event into Facebook.

So the ZM app acts as the bridge to Facebook, but the register/deposit info itself has to first come from the offer side.

Without that postback connection, the ZM app won’t know when a user registered or deposited.
